Process State Transition Simulator

Create processes and move them through the five states an operating system tracks: New, Ready, Running, Waiting and Terminated. Every move is checked against the real transition rules, and every attempt, valid or not, is written to the event log.

State diagram

New
The process is being created and is not yet competing for the CPU.
Ready
Loaded in memory and waiting in the ready queue for the scheduler.
Running
Its instructions are executing on the CPU.
Waiting
Blocked until an I/O operation or event completes.
Terminated
Finished executing. The OS releases its resources.
